Adult Formation Offering in October 2026
Adult Sunday School Hour 9:45-10:45 a.m.
Meeting in the Multi-Purpose Room Downstairs:
Our New Adventure continues in the Multi-Purpose Room (MPR)!  Walk with us as we explore our stories in the context of The Story God has been writing for all of time.  We will be seeking connection to each other through shared lectures, music, poetry, prayer, and responsive conversations. We want to dig deeper as a community as we talk about our own foundational experiences and seek to build a shared tapestry of understanding more about ourselves, our place in The Body, and our God who has made all things well.  Come ready to listen, come ready to speak, come ready to grow.
