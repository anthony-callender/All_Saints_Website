Sick Child Nursery Policy
While we welcome the opportunity to play with and pray with your child, we respectfully request that you do NOT bring your child to the nurseries when he or she:
has a fever (children must be fever-free for 24 hours without the use of fever-reducing medicine before being allowed into the nurseries)
has vomited in the past 12 hours
has had diarrhea in the past 12 hours
has nasal drainage that is not clear
is excessively coughing/hacking or sneezing
has an infection and has not been on an antibiotic for 24 hours
has a rash or skin infection
is lethargic and not feeling well, or seems to be coming down with something
You may be called on to pick up your child if the nursery staff feels your child is sick.  Thanks for helping us keep our youngest saints healthy!
