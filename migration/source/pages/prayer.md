# Prayer

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/prayer

Prayer Ministry
“
Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.
”
— Philippians 4:6
All Saints’ Church has been called by God to be a “House of Prayer.” We welcome your prayer requests regarding any and all concerns or praise reports for you, your family members or friends.
Prayer Requests
All prayer requests will be prayed for by the intercessory team for two weeks. Please be specific about your request (e.g. healing of cancer, etc.) and, if submitting for someone else, please note your relationship to this person, (e.g. yourself or your mother, son, uncle, cousin, neighbor, co-worker, etc.) Please note, we would love to receive a follow-up report, whether more prayer is needed or your prayers are answered. Contact Deacon Julie (
jtilton@allsaintswoodbridge.org
) with your feedback or submit a
praise report online
. We love to give God praise for answered prayer!

Other Ways to Receive Prayer
During the Prayers of the People, there will be a time for the congregation to participate aloud by lifting up personal names.
People in need of prayer are always encouraged to seek prayer from prayer teams located at the back of the church during Communion.
