# Serving Those in Need

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/serving-those-in-need

Hilda Barg Homeless Prevention Center
The Hilda Barg Homeless Prevention Center
, operated by Northern Virginia Family Services, is a 30-bed family shelter open 24/7 year-round. Services include: Family Emergency Shelter, Case Management, Housing Location Services, and Emergency Food Assistance.
Location: 14945 Jefferson Davis Hwy., Woodbridge
