Food Giveaways have been a monthly occurrence at All Saints’ Church since March of 2021. What makes the Food Giveaways unique to those who come through is the variety of fresh produce, meat, bread, and milk. Additionally, guests can choose from the nonperishable or canned goods, ensuring they get the food that best suits their family’s needs. The Food Giveaways also offer prayer in the Sanctuary.
