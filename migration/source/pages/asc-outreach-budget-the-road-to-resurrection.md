The Road to Resurrection began in 2021 as an outreach to the community during Holy Week, leading up to Easter. There are 13 costumed scenes presented from the Bible. Participants get to walk around the property to experience each scene leading up to the death and resurrection of Jesus. This family- friendly event has live animals, a petting zoo, crafts, trikes, an Easter egg hunt, and door prizes!
