The Art of Parenting: Aiming Your Child’s Heart Toward God
The years are short, but the days are long. All parents need encouragement along the way. Experience Adult Sunday School with
FAMILY LIFE'S Art of Parenting!
This 8-session, video-based study is designed to not only encourage and find new ways to integrate faith into everyday parenting moments, but also to create an environment where moms and dads can shoulder burdens together, celebrate breakthroughs, and relate to one another’s day-to-day parenting journey. Parents of all ages & stages invited!
Inspired by Psalm 127:4, the teaching sessions and conversations will cover the following topics:
The Goal of Parenting
Forming Character
Applying Discipline
Building Relationships
Understanding Identity
Nurturing Identity
Preparing for Mission
The Power of Family
How to Join In!
