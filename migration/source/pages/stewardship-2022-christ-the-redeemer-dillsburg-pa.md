Christ the Redeemer Anglican Church in Dillsburg, PA
is a church plant in the foothills of the Blue Ridge Mountains. Christ the Redeemer seeks to help people love God with their whole lives and to express Christ’s light to their community. The core values of loving God, yielding our life to Him, and sharing His light, guide us in our discipleship and mission. We experience these values by being anchored in scripture, praying together, experiencing channels of grace, living out historic practices, and serving people.
