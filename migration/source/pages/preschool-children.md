Space Place Preschool VBS
is just for Pre-K kids who have not yet completed Kindergarten! (Registration priority for volunteers; 30 slots available). Little ones gather with the older kids for Sing & Play Blast-Off (except Day 1 so they can immediately connect with their Crew Leaders & Space Place Preschool Area), experience a little less structure and more free-choice play in a simpler learning environment, enjoy Pre-K Space Place decorations, play on the playground, get messy with irresistible craft and play activities, and have their own interactive Bible Adventure! Nursery available for trained, volunteer parents.
