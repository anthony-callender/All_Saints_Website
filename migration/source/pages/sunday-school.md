Sunday School
Upon turning three, a child may "graduate" from the Toddler Nursery to the Pre-Kindergarten classroom. Sunday School is offered from 9:45-10:45 a.m. for ages Pre-K through the fifth grade in the Children's Wing. Colorful classrooms provide a welcoming setting for children to engage in a small group experience. While exploring the truths of God’s word, children play games, create crafts, enjoy a snack, pray and praise God together!
