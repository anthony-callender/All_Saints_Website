We’re looking for volunteers to join our Flower Guild, which helps beautify the sanctuary and building with both artificial and fresh flowers throughout the liturgical year.
We’re also looking for one volunteer in particular who would take the lead in scheduling a rota of people to serve approximately every other month.
If you’re interested in serving All Saints’ in this way, please email Father Scott at sbailey@allsaintswoodbridge.org.
