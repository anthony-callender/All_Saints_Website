# Give

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/giving

There are multiple ways to make a financial gift to All Saints’ Church. You can mail a check to the church (14851 Gideon Drive Woodbridge, VA 22192) or drop off a check at the church office. You can also submit your donation online or use “text” to make your donation!
Online givers now have the option to increase their donation amount in order to help offset costs for our church. The donor is always credited the full amount given. For instance, someone making a $100 credit card donation will have the option to add $3.30 to their donation. That person will be credited with a $103.30 contribution. For an ACH bank transfer directly from a checking account, the processing fee for $100 is less at $1.26.
