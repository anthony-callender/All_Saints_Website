# Sunday Services

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/sunday-services

Sunday Morning Services:
8:15 a.m. & 11 a.m.
Almighty God, to you all hearts are open, all desires known, and from you no secrets are hid:
Cleanse the thoughts of our hearts by the inspiration of your Holy Spirit,
that we may perfectly love you, and worthily magnify your holy Name;
through Christ our Lord.
Amen.
The Collect for Purity,
Book of Common Prayer
(p. 123)
All are invited to worship with us in our Anglican tradition of Holy Eucharist each Sunday at 8:15 a.m. or 11 a.m. Our services generally last 90 minutes; nursery care and Children’s Church are available during both. We include a mix of contemporary music and hymns in our worship. Our prayers are found in the
Book of Common Prayer
(2019). Take a look at the order of service for Sundays
here.
You can expect to see a range of dress from casual to formal. Visitors are welcome to dress as they are most comfortable attending a Sunday morning service. However you choose to join us, you will be welcome!

Children’s Church is offered at both services for children age 3 through 5th grade. They can be checked into the Children’s Wing before the service starts. Children are brought back into the Sanctuary after the sermon for Holy Eucharist.
Sunday school Hour
Sunday School Hour is on Sundays from 9:45 a.m. - 10:45 a.m. (between the services). We study various aspects of the Bible or biblical principles. Course topics vary throughout the seasons. Children (PreK through 12th grade) also participate in this learning opportunity, attending Sunday School during Sunday School Hour.
Nursery Care
Nursery care
is available during both services and during Sunday School Hour.
Building Accessibility
We want everyone to have access to our services, therefore our building has:
Accessible parking convenient to front and rear entrances.
Ramps from street to curb level with no additional steps to enter the building.
Accessible bathrooms (grab rails, larger stalls and angled mirrors).
Exterior doors that, when fully extended, will lock in the open position, allowing a guest to enter without having to hold the door open.
We would be happy to address any questions or concerns you might have. Please feel free to call our main office at 703-670-0093.
