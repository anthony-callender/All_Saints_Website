MomCo starts Monday, September 14th, 10 a.m. - 12 noon, with nursery care!
MomCo meets twice a month, is a Christian moms group for any mom with children of any age, and all ages are invited. Moms will meet with coffee & breakfast refreshments in the Children's Wing (Heaven Room) while hearing about this year's theme. Virginia Balach returns as this year's Coordinator, and is excited to incorporate official MomCo Mama Meetup materials in weekly meetings to help moms in their faith journey.  And, of course, plenty of time to take a break with other mom friends.  Nursery-age children are lovingly cared for by church members in the church nursery.
