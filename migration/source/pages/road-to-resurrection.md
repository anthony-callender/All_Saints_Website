# Road to Resurrection

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/road-to-resurrection

Road to Resurrection & Community Easter Egg Hunt
SATURDAY, APRIL 4, 2026 10 A.M. - 1 P.M.
2026 road to resurrection program booklet
Come experience this family outdoor event for all ages,
filled with powerful moments that surround the death and resurrection of Jesus.
You are invited to a family and community outdoor Easter event filled with
Bible-time experiences
that will engage participants of all ages, right here on church grounds.
During this event, your family will travel the path Jesus journeyed. Travel with Jesus on a donkey through the praising streets of Jerusalem, sit down for the Last Supper, pray in the Garden of Gethsemane, discover the sacrifice of Good Friday, and celebrate freedom from sin at the empty tomb that first Easter morning!
Walk through at your own pace, with your family, or in a small group. At each destination, experience powerful moments that surround the death and resurrection of Jesus.
End with a celebration of the new life we have in Christ with an Easter Egg Hunt for children ages toddler to 11 years old!
Road to Resurrection takes place from 10 a.m. - 1 p.m.
