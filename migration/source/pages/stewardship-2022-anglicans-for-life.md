Anglicans for Life (AFL)
works to inspire every believer in every church to do one thing every day to protect life and prevent abortion and euthanasia. We work to eradicate abortion and euthanasia through ministry, education, pastoral care, and advocacy. Through annual events like the March for Life, AFL provides educational and advocacy opportunities for churches and parishioners to promote life-affirming action.
