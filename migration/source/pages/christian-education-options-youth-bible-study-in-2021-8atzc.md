In 2021 we continue our weekly Bible studies on Thursday nights via Zoom. We are using the excellent resources from The Bible Project (TBP). I'm excited to share that TBP launched a new series of Bible studies titled “Reflections” that includes a companion podcast for each Bible study. The Bible studies consist of a Bible Project video, a short audio message, Scripture readings, and discussion questions. The podcast is great if you're looking to go deeper after the Bible study. It's perfect for individual meditation on the passages covered in the weekly Bible study. Click
