Happy Tuesday, everyone!
I want to share with you the details about the youth watching the Super Bowl this Sunday.
- Students can arrive at the multi purpose room, with food, drinks, and snacks at 6pm.
- Kick-off is scheduled for 6:30.
- We will have several youth leaders present throughout the evening.
- Students will text their parents to come pick them up by 10pm. If a student would like to leave earlier, that's fine. That student will still text a parent to come pick him/her up.
- For students not interested in watching the game, but interested in hanging out and socializing, there will be uno, board games, other card games, table tennis, and fuseball going on. Students can bring a game to play as well.
It will be a fun night with good food, good games, and perhaps a good football game!
