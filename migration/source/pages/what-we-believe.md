What We Believe
All Saints’ Church is rooted in the historic interpretation of the Christian Bible—which is summarized in the words of the Apostles' and Nicene Creeds. Our specific theological convictions are expressed in the Thirty-Nine Articles of the Anglican Communion. All Saints' Church is part of the
Anglican Church in North America
and is under Bishop Chris Warner of the
Diocese of the Mid-Atlantic
.
Each Sunday we confess our faith in the words of the Nicene Creed.
