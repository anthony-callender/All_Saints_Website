At
Common Recovery
our goal is to
shower Prince William County with a message of hope; cultivating a community of recovery through recovery services, spiritual growth and peer housing. Evangelizing the working poor and raising up effective leaders from the least, the last and the lost.
This  program is known for its message of recovery for those who are hopelessly struggling with alcohol/drug addiction and have found no other way out.
It is our desire to reach out to those who are still suffering and lost.
We are here to pass on what was freely given to us —
“a message of hope.”
We were once on a path of destruction ourselves – addicted to alcohol and drugs. Alone in that dark pit of misery; we were desperate for change; but not able to stop! Our hearts cry was that God would stop the madness! That He would deliver us from that lifestyle that only promised death.
As recovered addicts ourselves, we understand & can help. It’s about staying clean & sober but that’s not all there’s more– it’s about getting our lives back as well!
