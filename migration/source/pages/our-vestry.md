Our Vestry
The Vestry currently consists of the Rector and nine elected lay members. Each elected lay member serves a three-year term. The Vestry provides oversight in the temporal activities, business affairs, and property of the Church; assists the Rector in providing spiritual leadership and oversight of the ecclesiastical affairs of the Church; prays regularly for the Church and its mission; and serves as a resource for godly counsel, biblical wisdom, and accountability for the Rector and other clergy of the Church.
