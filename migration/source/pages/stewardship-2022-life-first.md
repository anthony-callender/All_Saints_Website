Life First
provides medical services, pregnancy tests, ultrasound pregnancy confirmation, and classroom instruction. Additional support includes prenatal and parenting education, sexual integrity classes, and abortion recovery Bible studies. They also share the Gospel of Jesus Christ with each client. They now serve the community at two locations in Manassas and Woodbridge.
