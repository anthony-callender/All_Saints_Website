Rocky Railway VBS - Coming this Summer!
Emily Fawcett
May 1, 2020
Our Vacation Bible School theme this year is just the ticket in all of our lives as we adjust to new schedules, changes, trials, and transitions. In anticipation that the church will re-open again at least sometime this summer, we are preparing for a faith-filled adventure where kids discover that Jesus's power pulls us through! After prayerful planning, we believe a July or August, on-site VBS is the best approach this year, and plans are in the works to make it an event to remember! Children and their friends will track through Bible Adventures, explore science and crafts at Imagination Station, worship at Sing & Play Express, experience other energetic Railroad stations, and of course romp in the church backyard in Locomotion Games. More details coming soon!
