Men’s Ministry
All Saints’ Church Men’s Ministry seeks to promote (1) the spiritual growth of men, (2) building up the role of men in their families, (3) being intergenerational, and (4) having a service component with existing and new ministries in the church.  The Men’s Ministry is led by a leadership team of men who organize its efforts pursuant to these objectives. Two activities to be aware of are coffee hours on the third Sunday of each month and bi-monthly Men’s Prayer Breakfasts on the third Saturday of each odd (i.e. January, March, etc) month.
Please look for announcements and information on the church website, weekly Newsletter, monthly Fridge Sheets, and emails from Men’s Ministry leadership.

: An Encouragement for Daily Bible Reading
All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work.
(2 Timothy 3:16-17)
Daily reading of the Bible has so many benefits. We learn. We are cautioned. We are corrected and/or redirected. We are trained in what God says is right. We are equipped for all good works God has prepared in advance for us to do (Ephesians 2:10). Reading Scripture and asking God for the wisdom to apply it in our everyday life ultimately helps us to become the disciples Jesus commands us to be in the Great Commission (Matthew 28:18-19).
Nowhere in the Bible does God say how much to read or hear each time we look into His Scripture. So whether you read (or listen to) a little or a lot of Scripture on a daily basis, please do make it a habit.
May you receive the greatest benefit of all: knowing God and drawing closer to Him.

Mission

Renew and reinstitute Men’s Ministry that will have as its objective (1) the spiritual growth of men, (2) building up the role of men in their families, (3) being intergenerational, and (4) having a service component with existing and new ministries in the church.
