Thursday Evenings (7-8:15 p.m.) beginning September 22 at Panera, 4402 Fortuna Center Plaza, Dumfries
That You May Believe: A Study of the Gospel of John
This women’s study will meet at Panera on Thursday Evenings from 7-8:15 for a time of fellowship and study of this beloved gospel. John tells his readers why he wrote his account – that you “may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the son of God,” and “have life in His name.” No matter where you are in your faith journey, come and let the wonderful truths of this book minister to you. Bring your Bible and a journal or notebook. Group time will start at 7:00 p.m. so please get your snack or drink ahead of that time. All are welcome!
