Children’s Ministry
“...so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith—that you, being rooted and grounded in love...
— Ephesians 3:17
The Children’s Ministry at All Saints’ Church serves infants, toddlers, and children through the fifth grade. While we recognize and affirm that the spiritual development of children is first and foremost a parent’s responsibility, our church is poised and ready to assist! We seek to partner with you and support your family in training up children to love and serve the Lord each day of their lives. Children are welcomed, valued, protected, and lovingly cared for while they learn who Jesus is, how to follow Him and how the truths of the Bible can help them to make Godly choices. Our dedicated Children’s Ministry team is committed to helping children:
Understand that God earnestly desires a personal, meaningful and growing relationship through Jesus with them
Read, know, and love God’s word
Learn how to engage in daily prayer and worship
Experience God’s presence through the Holy Spirit as an integral part of their lives
Voluntarily participate in acts of service
Become committed to using their spiritual gifts
Grow in Christ-like character
