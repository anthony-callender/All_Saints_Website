After serving in Uganda for 10 1/2  years, the Rev. Dr. Jessica Hughes is on an extended medical and study leave while preparing to provide spiritual direction to missionaries (and others). She is an intern in the Selah spiritual direction certificate program, and is using her technical background to assist SAMS (Society of Anglican Missionaries and Senders) with choosing new donor management software.
