“Acts”
The Spirit moved in dramatic ways in the early church. The Gospel was preached, thousands were added to their number, many were healed, and the good news about Jesus was spread across continents. Covering the exciting action of the Spirit in the years just following Jesus' life on earth, this study will open your eyes to the Spirit’s power in the past and the present and help you apply the riches of Scripture to your own life.
“Acts” by John Stott is a book small group augmented by video segments and will meet in the Creation Room of the Children’s Wing. Study guides will be available on the first day of class for $12 each (Kindle version is $10.49 if you choose to order on your own). Coffee/Tea will be provided.
