# About Stellar VBS

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/about-vbs

Stellar Vacation Bible School
VBS This Summer!
July 24-28, 9 a.m. - 12 Noon
Shining Jesus’ Light to the World!
This is THE summer event that launches kids on a cosmic quest shining Jesus’ light to the world.  Stellar VBS rockets kids on an out-of-this-world adventure that’s lightyears of faith-building fun. Kids explore how Jesus shines hope, love, forgiveness, and joy to the world, and how we can all do the same with His power!
Small group crews and their Crew Leader will travel together for each day’s adventure in a week full of faith discoveries at Stellar Bible Adventures, worship at Sing & Play Blast-off, play outdoor “All-Star” Games, eat fun treats at Galactic Snacks, view Kid-Vid Cinema real-kid testimonies, and experimenting and creating at Imagination Station! Children who have graduated kindergarten-5th grade are welcome to register. 6th graders and up can serve as Crew Leaders or Assistants!
