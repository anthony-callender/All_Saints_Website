# Serve in the Community

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/serve-in-the-community

Serving Our Neighbors
Food Giveaway
Our mission is simple: We demonstrate God’s love to ANYONE in need by providing nutritious food in a dignified, respectful, and timely manner.
Hilda Barg Homeless Prevention Center
All Saints' works in partnership with Hilda Barg to provide meals for one week, twice a year.
Prison Ministry
Homemade cookies are a tangible way that the team shares God’s love and concern to those who seldom encounter either in the course of their daily lives. Approximately 30,000 cookies are needed for each Kairos weekend.
Dorcas Ministry
We create, bless, and collect completed items for distribution to the poor, the sick, and the needy.
