# Timeline

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/timeline

Timeline
All Saints’ roots date back to the first Anglican church established in Prince William County in 1667 when a chapel was built on the banks of Quantico Creek in Dumfries.
January 1, 1980
St. Martin's, a mission church in the Dumfries-Quantico area started in 1955 by Trinity Episcopal in Manassas, and Holy Spirit, a mission church in Dale City started in 1963 by St. Margaret’s, merge to form All Saints’. The bishop proposed the merger since St. Martin’s had a building, but no priest, while Holy Spirit had a priest, but no building. Conditions of the merger included calling a new vicar and building a new church in Dale City. The Rev. Chuck Stein, who had been vicar of Holy Spirit, continued as interim vicar of the combined congregations until he retired on Dec. 31, 1980. Paula Woods and Robert Pritchard followed as interim vicars.
Feb. 17, 1980
All Saints’ holds its first service at the old St. Martin’s building in Triangle.
December 1981
The Rev. John A.M. Guernsey called as All Saints’ new vicar
March 3, 1983
All Saints’ holds its first service in its new building on Saratoga Lane in Dale City
April 10, 1983
All Saints’ is dedicated
AUGUST 1985
The Rev. Whis Hays and the Rev. Mary Hays join All Saints’ as associate vicars
SEPTEMBER 1987
Mary Hays leaves for Truro Church in Fairfax
Jan. 31, 1988
All Saints’ gains full parish status
JUNE 1989
The Rev. Valarie Whitcomb joins All Saints’ as associate rector
SEPTEMBER 1989
Whis Hayes leaves All Saints’ to start Rock the World Youth Mission Alliance in Ambridge, PA
1995
The original prayer room built as part of a six-room expansion
May 2001
All Saints’ purchases 28 acres of land on Gideon Drive in Woodbridge next to Hylton Chapel to build a new, larger church to hold the growing congregation
NOV. 2005
The Rev. Mark Brown joins All Saints’ as associate rector
Dec. 10, 2006
Congregation votes to leave The Episcopal Church, becoming part of the Anglican Diocese of North Kigezi within the Church of Uganda
Sept. 2007
John Guernsey consecrated as bishop, taking on new diocesan duties while remaining All Saints’ rector
June 23, 2009
All Saints’ transfers out of the Church of Uganda and into the newly established Anglican Church in North America
SEPTEMBER 2009
Valarie Whitcomb retires as associate rector
April 2010
The Rev. Dan Morgan called to become All Saints’ next rector
June 13, 2010
John Guernsey officially steps down as All Saints’ rector after 28 years to devote all his attention as the bishop of the then-Diocese of the Holy Spirit in the Anglican Church of North America
APRIL 1, 2011
The Rev. Alex Leighton joins All Saints’ as associate rector
September 2011
New All Saints’ church opens on Gideon Drive
JUNE 2015
Mark Brown retires as associate rector
JUNE 2018
Alex Leighton leaves All Saints’ to plant a church in Bozeman, Montana
August 31, 2018
Dan Morgan leaves All Saints’ to become rector at St. Luke's in Copley, Ohio
March 7, 2019
The Rev. Scott Bailey called as All Saints’ next rector
JAN, 2021
The Rev. Jedd Trenum becomes All Saints’ associate rector
DEC. 2025
The Rev. Maryanna Stumbaugh becomes All Saints’ associate rector. She replaces Jedd Trenum, who  became vicar at The Anglican Church of the Valley in Staunton, VA.
AUG. 2026
The Rev. Patricio Rojas joins All Saints’ staff as the associate rector for Spanish-Language Ministry.
