Foundations for Ministry, Year 2
begins via Zoom on Sunday evening, September 13, at 6 p.m. This is the second year of a two-year course and the group will be studying the New Testament.
If you have taken Year 1 or are a Foundations Alumni who would like a disciplined study, you are invited to register!
The study is designed to equip participants to meet the opportunities for ministry in the Church and in the world through the study of God's Word.  Groups meet from 6 to 8 p.m. each Sunday evening during the school year.  We will be meeting via Zoom until it is safe to meet in person.
