In preparation for our November 1, 2020 visit by Bishop John Guernsey, we will be providing a seven-week Confirmation Preparation class via Zoom for those desiring to be confirmed or received into our church.  To stand for Confirmation, Reception, or Reaffirmation, you must 16 or older, baptized under a Trinitarian formula (i.e. “In the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit”), complete this class, and attend a rehearsal on Saturday, October 31.  Classes will be led by Deacon Andy and Father Jedd Trenum via Zoom on Tuesday evenings at 7 p.m. beginning September 15. Those interested should register below. If you have further questions, please contact Desiree Barker by phone or email at the church office, 703-670-0093 or at
