Work alongside fellow parishioners to help beautify our church inside and out!
This is an opportunity to use your hands as a love offering to the ministry and outreach of All Saints' Church.
Let’s make our church as attractive as it can be, for parishioners and newcomers alike.
Coffee and light snacks will be offered in the Atrium for volunteers as well as pizza and beverages near the lunch hour.
Please
sign up here
as soon as possible! We want to plan well so that we might accomplish well all of our goals.
We also need six captains for outside tasks. You can indicate your interest on the
sign-up form
.
The specific work we’ll do includes both indoor and outdoor tasks.
Outside:
— Spreading compost on lawn areas adjacent to the front and side of the Church building.
— Weeding and caring for flower and plant beds.
— Tree-trimming in and near the Church building and in the parking islands.
— Sweeping up/blowing debris as work occurs.
— Gideon Drive litter pickup.
Inside:
— Inside maintenance tasks (at the Jr. Warden’s direction)
— Dusting sanctuary kneelers and aligning pew seats.
— Organizing storage rooms as needed.
— Coffee and snacks preparation and distribution
