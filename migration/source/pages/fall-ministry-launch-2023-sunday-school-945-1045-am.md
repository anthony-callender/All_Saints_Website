Pre-K Class & Elementary Class
The ‘Simply Loved’ curriculum will continue this fall for ages 3 through 6th grade, as children explore the New and Old Testaments, and discover friendship with Jesus throughout God’s story. Children will participate in worship songs, meet a Bible Buddy that ties Bible points to animal facts, learn new Bible stories, and participate in a hands-on activity or game.
Our children are Simply Loved as they are engaged with relational interaction, conversation, and teachings to understand Bible truths and trigger scripture memory, while all pointing to their own friendship with Jesus.
