Vacation Bible School is open to all children in the community, and special invitations are made to those attending our monthly Food Giveaways and Story Time at the Park. The format of Vacation Bible School at All Saints’ Church allows children to be in small groups (called crews) with whom they travel around the building all week! Bible points for each day make the Gospel message easy to understand for children who don’t attend a local church. VBS volunteers serve in all different areas throughout the week, and members of the community are welcomed into our beautiful building.
