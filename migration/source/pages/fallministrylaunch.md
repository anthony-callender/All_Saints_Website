Fall Ministry Kickoff - 2023
Education & Ministry Opportunities For All Ages!
Dear All Saints’ Church Family,
As we approach a new season, we are excited to share new ministry opportunities for all ages launching this fall. There are opportunities for every single one of us to grow in our walk with Christ regardless of age.
Let me challenge each of you to prayerfully consider what education and ministry opportunities God is calling you to be a part of this fall to grow and mature in your walk with Him. As together we follow his leading, my prayer is that we all become more mature and fully committed disciples of Jesus.
Together with you in His service,
