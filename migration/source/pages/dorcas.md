# Dorcas (Needlework) Ministry

Source: https://www.allsaintswoodbridge.org/dorcas

The Dorcas Ministry
The Dorcas Ministry
meets on the first Saturday of the month, October through June, in the middle school classroom (lower level in the right back corner of the Multi-Purpose Room) from 9:30-11 a.m. Bring crocheting, knitting, sewing, and other items to work on while we are gathered, as well as items you have completed. We will bless and collect completed items for distribution to the poor, the sick, and the needy.
Why “
Dorcas Ministry”
?
Dorcas was a disciple of Jesus and lived in the town of Joppa, a city on the coast of the Mediterranean Sea. She was a charitable person who made things for the needy. You can read about her in
Acts 9:36-42
, including Peter’s restoration of her life.
