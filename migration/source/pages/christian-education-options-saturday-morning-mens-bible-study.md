Men of All Saints’ meet each Saturday morning from 8-9 a.m. via Zoom to study God’s Word together. If you are interested in going deeper in your Bible study join the class at any time. There is no homework, just a great opportunity to study together. The Zoom link is available in the weekly email that outlines the Zoom offerings for the week.
