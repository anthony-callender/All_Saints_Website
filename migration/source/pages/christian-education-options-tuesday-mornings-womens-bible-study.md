Tuesday Morning (9:45- 11:30 a.m.)
That You May Believe: A Study of the Gospel of John-
This women’s study will meet at the church on Tuesday mornings for a time of fellowship and study of this beloved gospel. John tells his readers why he wrote his account – that you “may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the son of God,” and “have life in His name.”
No matter where you are in your faith journey, come and let the wonderful truths of this book minister to you. Bring your Bible and a journal or notebook. The group will gather in the Admin. Conference Room at 9:45 for fellowship and start promptly at 10 am. Childcare TBD, please contact
