When the Soul Listens: Finding Rest and Direction in Contemplative Prayer
This book study “provides a solid biblical, theological and historical grounding for one of the most important practices in the Christian life: contemplative prayer.” We will work our way through the text, the practices, and the discussion both as a group and on our own. If this is an area you would like to explore or if you seek to move to a deeper level in this area, plan to join this group. The book,
When the Soul Listens
by Jan Johnson is $14.00 and available the first day the group meets. This group will meet weekly in the Bishop’s Conference Room (on the upper level of the Admin. Wing)
