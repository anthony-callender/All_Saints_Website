The Anglican Communion New Wineskins Missionary Network is dedicated to raising awareness of Anglicans to the biblical call to fulfill the Great Commission and the task of world evangelism through training people to serve as missionaries around the globe, equipping Anglicans to witness for Jesus Christ in cultures with no viable Christian church, and undergirding all mission work with prayer.
