Inspired by God’s radical grace, the Anglican Relief and Development Fund exists to empower Anglicans in the developing world to show the love of Christ to those in need in their own communities: Clean water for the thirsty, jobs for the poor, education for children, food for the hungry, and hope for those without hope.
