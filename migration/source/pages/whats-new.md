Children's Ministry Parking Lot Picnic
Friday, May 15, from Noon-1 p.m, at Southlake Park, Montclair/Dumfries, 5070 Higgins Drive. Pack a picnic lunch (or pickup Chik-Fil-A!), load up the kids, and let's enjoy a hangout out of the hatchback of our cars, distanced within the bounds of our own vehicles, while waving and distantly visiting with All Saints’ Church families and friends. Bring a lawnchair for you, and the kids can sit in the open area of your vehicle! Bring a stroller for the littles. We will keep this no more than the hour so wiggly children can move on with their day.  Let's Lunch!
Your Journey from Mourning to Joy
A new GriefShare online class from 6-8 p.m. is beginning Monday June 10 and ending 13 weeks later on Monday, August 24. The Rev. Julie Tilton will facilitate each class. GriefShare is designed to help people grieving the loss of a loved one. GriefShare’s content and its three-component design (video seminar, small group discussion, and workbook) work together to help participants along that journey.

All Saints’ Church Serving in our community
Members of All Saints' Church are using their gifts to bless first responders, family, and friends in our community! Our Staff and Vestry Members have received them also, along with a grouping for our children, should they need them, when they return to Sunday School!

Prayer After Sunday Services
For one hour following our online Sunday worship services (11 a.m. – Noon), Prayer Team members will be available to pray with All Saints’ parishioners. This is our means of continuing the ministry normally offered by Prayer Teams during communion. Anyone who would like to receive prayer over the phone, please contact us by sending an e-mail to
prayerteams@allsaintswoodbridge.org
. Provide your request at any time, however you will only be contacted between 11 a.m. and noon on Sundays. This prayer will be short and focused, just as it has been at the back of the church during communion. All are encouraged to send prayer requests to the All Saints’ Church Prayer Center online via the front page of our website and designated prayer personnel affiliated with our Prayer Center will pray for you as well.

Help for the Homebound
If you are among the high-risk population who must remain home but need groceries, prescriptions, or any other assistance, please let us know—your church family will take care of you. Email the church office at
office@allsaintswoodbridge.org
with your need and someone from our Caring Ministries team will get back with you.
Conversely…
If you are not among the high-risk population and are available to run errands for members of our church family who cannot leave home, please email the same address with your contact information and we will add you to our list of volunteers. And THANK YOU for that.
