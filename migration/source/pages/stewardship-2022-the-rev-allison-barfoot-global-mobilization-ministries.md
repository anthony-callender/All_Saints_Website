The Rev. Canon Alison L. Barfoot serves as the Assistant for International Relations to the Archbishop of Uganda. This ministry represents the cutting edge of mission strategy in the church today, for Uganda is not only a mission field—it is also an incredibly ripe mission force with which we can partner to fulfill the Great Commission (Matt. 28:16-20). The focus of Alison’s work is to mobilize Ugandans to bless the nations.
