In a series on the Old Testament Minor Prophets, we will be examining the Book of Hosea. The prophet Hosea speaks volumes to us as Christians about our relationship with God and others, God's heart for His people, their future and the Gospel itself.
When: Class meets every Saturday Morning at 8:00 AM, beginning September 18.
Where: All Saint’s Church, Sanctuary.
