All nursery and elementary children invited to a weekly Story Time on Wednesdays at 10 a.m. virtually or in the church backyard! Come enjoy an All Saints’ friend read a quality children's story to your children, enjoy a playful activity or game, and then playtime and friendship on the playground! Parents are asked to please stay and enjoy the fun.
When meeting virtually, please bring masks and a lawn chair. We will ensure social distancing.
Invite a friend!
