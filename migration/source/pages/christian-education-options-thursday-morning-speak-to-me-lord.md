Experience the Father putting His arms around you as He speaks to you. He is the one who knows you so well that you have nothing left to hide. He loves you, warts and all. He wants a relationship with You and is waiting for you to stop and listen, to slow down and make space for Him. To listen intently and intentionally as you read His Words. This is not a “how to” class. This is a “do it” class.  This is not “group work.” This is “personal work.” Come join your sisters on Thursday mornings beginning September 17 from 10 a.m. - noon for some in-depth God time. Be ready to receive security, freedom, healing, and intimacy with your heavenly Father. Come just as you are. No prerequisites required! If you need further information, or would like to join this group, please email Deacon Julie Tilton at
