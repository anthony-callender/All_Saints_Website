“Cheap grace is the deadly enemy of our Church. We are fighting to-day for costly grace.”
“When Christ calls a man, he bids him come and die.”
Considered a classic, Dietrich Bonhoeffer’s
The Cost of Discipleship
wrestles with the question of what it means to actually live the faith we proclaim as Christians.
In Bonhoeffer’s case, it meant execution at the hands of the Nazi regime during World War II.
The Cost of Discipleship
is available for purchase from the All Saints’ Bookshelf, located in the Atrium, for $14. Payment can be made by cash or check, or by credit card via an online form. See the Bookshelf for details.
