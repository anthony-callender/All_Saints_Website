Story Time in the Park is a tangible way that All Saints’ Church is reaching the surrounding Dale City Community families.
Starting in 2020 with various locations, Story Time now takes place every Thursday at Cloverdale Park.
Our Children’s Ministry Director, Tara Pedersen, has formed relationships with the moms, dads, and children who come — parents often join in and participate as well. These relationships have led to families attending (and serving) in Vacation Bible School and participating in Sunday morning activities.
Story Time in the Park promotes literacy and the benefits of reading to children. It also provides snacks and an educational craft for all children who attend.
