Kairos is similar to Cursillo or Walk to Emmaus. It includes a three-and-a-half-day weekend program where a trained team of Christians go into a prison to carry God’s message of love and forgiveness to those incarcerated. In October 2024, All Saints’ Church sent a team to the Central Virginia Correctional Unit for Women.
