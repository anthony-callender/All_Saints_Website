The Holy Spirit: The Lord, the Giver of Life
We invite you to come explore the Person and work of the Holy Spirit. Each week we will look at a different aspect of the Spirit as He is revealed in both the Old and New Testaments. We will also explore the work of the Spirit in the life of the believer. How does He operate in our life as a community and in our worship?  What are spiritual gifts and what place do they have in each believer’s life? These questions and others will be addressed over this 10-week course. Class will meet in the Sanctuary.
