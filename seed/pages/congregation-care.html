There are so many ways to provide care to other members of our congregation. If you are interested in serving in any of these areas, please contact Deacon Julie Tilton through the church office at 703-670-0093.

Ways to care

  • Practical Assistance Coordinators (training provided)
  • Home Communion (training required)
  • Stephen Ministers (50 hours of training; two-year commitment; supervision)
  • Meals Ministry
  • Transportation (to church services/activities and doctor appointments)
  • Handyman services for the elderly and infirm
  • Support Groups
  • Prayer Ministry (training required)
  • Homebound and lonely visitation

Receiving prayer

Prayer requests are prayed for by the intercessory team for two weeks. During the Prayers of the People there is a time to lift up names aloud, and prayer teams are available at the back of the church during Communion. Share a prayer request.
