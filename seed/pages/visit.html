All are invited to worship with us in our Anglican tradition of Holy Eucharist each Sunday at 8:15 a.m. or 11 a.m. However you choose to join us, you will be welcome!

What to expect on Sunday

Our services generally last 90 minutes. We include a mix of contemporary music and hymns in our worship, and our prayers are found in the Book of Common Prayer (2019). You can look at the order of service ahead of time.

You can expect to see a range of dress from casual to formal. Visitors are welcome to dress as they are most comfortable.

Communion is offered every Sunday during both services and is open to all baptized believers, regardless of religious tradition. Baptized infants and children may take communion also.

Bringing children

  • Nursery (birth through age four) is open during both services and during the Sunday School Hour. On your first visit, stop by the Welcome/Check-in center just outside the Children’s Wing to register; parents and children receive matching ID bracelets.
  • Children’s Church is offered at both services for children age 3 through 5th grade. Children return to the service in time to receive communion or a blessing.
  • Sunday School for PreK through 12th grade meets 9:45–10:45 a.m., between the services.

Getting here

All Saints’ Church, 14851 Gideon Drive, Woodbridge, VA 22192. Get directions in Google Maps.

  • Accessible parking convenient to front and rear entrances.
  • Ramps from street to curb level with no additional steps to enter the building.
  • Accessible bathrooms (grab rails, larger stalls and angled mirrors).
  • Exterior doors that lock in the open position, so guests can enter without holding the door.

Can’t make it in person?

Sunday services are livestreamed on our YouTube channel, and recent preaching is in our sermon library.
