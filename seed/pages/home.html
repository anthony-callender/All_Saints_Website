First time at All Saints’?

All are invited to worship with us in our Anglican tradition of Holy Eucharist each Sunday. However you choose to join us, you will be welcome!

  • Dress ranges from casual to formal: come as you are most comfortable.
  • Nursery care, and Children’s Church for ages 3 through 5th grade, at both Sunday services.
  • Accessible parking, step-free entrances and accessible restrooms.
  • Communion is open to all baptized believers.
